The 5 dimensions五大伦理维度

HEART codes every benchmark against 5 ethical dimensions distilled from a corpus of 18 policy + governance sources (international guidelines, hard law, industry practice, surveys). The 5 dimensions trace back to a corporate-ethics framework that decomposes the same construct space; an internal "Open Co-Governance" 6th pillar is captured at the governance layer (T13/T14) rather than as a per-item label because it is structural. HEART 把每个 benchmark 按 5 大伦理维度打标。这 5 维由 18 份政策与治理文献蒸馏而来（国际指南、硬法律、行业实践、综述）。维度框架对应一套企业科技伦理准则；其中"开放共治"作为结构性治理维度由治理层工具 (T13/T14) 承接，未纳入逐条 benchmark 的标签。

Policy ⇄ Dimension Source Support政策 ⇄ 维度 来源支持矩阵

For each of 18 policy/governance sources we record the evidence type per dimension: D1 direct primary citation · D2 indirect (framing) · D3 tertiary (practice) · S survey / meta-review. 18 个政策/治理来源中，每条针对 5 维度记录支持类型：D1 直接一手引用 · D2 间接（动机/框架）· D3 三次（行业实践）· S 综述/元评论。
