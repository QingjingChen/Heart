How to read this

Bars show the share of the 103 audited benchmarks falling into each score bucket (0 – 5) for the corresponding rubric. Rubrics are grouped by the 3 layers used in the diagnostic codebook (Content Validity / Evaluation Design / Governance Reliability) and sorted by their mean score within each layer. R12 (Robustness vs gaming & contamination) and R14 (Maintenance & update governance) are the two layers with the worst score floor — most public benchmarks have no hidden split, no contamination guard, and no retirement policy. Click any bar to open the calibrated v2 anchor with concrete benchmark examples per score.
