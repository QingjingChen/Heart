How to read如何阅读

Rows = 14 rubrics (the diagnosis dimensions). Columns = 14 generic repair tools. A coloured cell means: when a bench fails the row's rubric, the column's tool is one repair option, and at least one bench in the corpus has shown this fix. Darker = stronger evidence. 行 = 14 条诊断 rubric。列 = 14 件通用修复工具。彩色格代表：当某 bench 在该 rubric 上有 gap，该列工具是一种可用修复方案，并且语料中至少有一个 bench 已展示过该修复。颜色越深 = 证据越强。
