⚠️ Worked examples, not empirical validation演示性 worked example，非实证验证
These 5 mini cases demonstrate how HEART moves from rubric diagnosis to repair operations. They are illustrative — they do not claim that the revised items have been empirically proven superior to the originals via model experiments or large-scale expert review. The intent is to show the workflow, not to publish a new benchmark. 这 5 个 mini case 演示 HEART 如何从 rubric 诊断走到修复操作。它们是演示性的——并不声称 revised items 已经被模型实验或大规模专家评审证明优于原题。目的是展示工作流，而非发布新 benchmark。
